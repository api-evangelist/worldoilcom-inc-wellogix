# WorldOil.com, Inc./Wellogix

> Wellogix (originally incorporated as WorldOil.com, Inc.) was a Houston, Texas software company serving the upstream oil and gas industry, backed by Battery Ventures. Its hosted tools — WorkFlowNavigator, DynaMaps, DrillTeam, and FieldTeam — helped drilling engineers plan wells and let operators and service companies collaborate on and procure complex engineered services. The company is no longer active: wellogix.com has registered DNS but no responding web server (probed 2026-07-21), and no public API, developer portal, SDKs, or packages were found. The worldoil.com domain today belongs to Gulf Publishing's World Oil magazine, a separate, unrelated business.
